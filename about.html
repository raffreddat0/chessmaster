01 / PROJECT

GOAL

The idea comes from the desire to feel again that unique sensation of physically touching and moving the pieces on the chessboard, an aspect that traditional digital play often fails to convey.

02 / MECHANICAL ARM

Four-bar Linkage

The mechanical arm relies on a wooden four-bar linkage powered by servomotors for fluid front-to-back motion. A dedicated vertical mechanism uses a motorized nylon thread to lower and align the electromagnet, allowing precise piece picking across the chessboard.

03 / DISPLAY

ChessOS.

The 20x4 LCD display and analog joystick form the user interface of ChessOS. They allow players to easily navigate menus, select game modes, configure system settings, and receive real-time feedback during matches.

Display LCD 20×4

HOW IT WORKS

Navigating ChessOS is intuitive: move the joystick to browse through menu options, press it to confirm a selection, and just have fun!
